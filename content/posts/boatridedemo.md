**Exciting News!** I am thrilled to announce a new update for the Boat Ride Project on the Unity Asset Store. This update brings some fantastic new features, enhancements, and a smoother gaming experience for all the enthusiasts out there.

## What's New in this Update?

1. **Universal Render Pipeline (URP) Support**: We've added full support for the Universal Render Pipeline, making your boat game look even more stunning with enhanced visuals and performance optimizations.

2. **Screen Orientation Support**: Now you can enjoy the game in both portrait and landscape modes, giving you the flexibility to play the way you prefer.

3. **New Shader Work**: We've been hard at work on our shaders, creating eye-catching visual effects that will take your boat game to the next level.
